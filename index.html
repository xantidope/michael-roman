About Me

The bridge between the boardroom and the array field

For over a decade I've worked as an independent technical representative for investors, developers and equipment manufacturers. My job is simple: make sure every watt promised on paper actually shows up in the field — safely, on schedule, and bankable.

From desert-scale utility plants to commercial rooftops, I translate engineering detail into decisions: reviewing designs, witnessing FAT/SAT, leading commissioning, hunting hotspots with thermography, and turning SCADA data into actionable performance reports.

What I Do

Engineering services, end to end

Independent, vendor-neutral expertise you can plug into any phase of your solar asset.

Site Assessment & Surveys

Shade analysis, irradiance review, structural & electrical feasibility, geotech coordination and bankable yield estimates.

PV Design & Review

String sizing, BOS specification, single-line diagrams, PVsyst simulation and independent design audits for lenders.

Owner's Engineer / QA-QC

Factory acceptance tests, construction supervision, punch-list management and IEC 62446-compliant acceptance reports.

Commissioning & Grid Connection

Cold/hot commissioning, protection relay checks, inverter parameterization and utility witnessing up to 132 kV.

Thermography & Diagnostics

Drone & handheld IR campaigns, I-V curve tracing, EL imaging review and PID / hotspot root-cause analysis.

Performance & O&M Advisory

PR / energy yield audits, SCADA analytics, warranty claims support and repowering studies for operating assets.
