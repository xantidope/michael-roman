Full split active. Expect 60–70% of forecast while the platforms collect data. No budget changes — protect the learning phase.

Months 2–3 · Optimization

$3,000

Switch off anything paying 1.5× too much per lead. Fresh creatives every 3 weeks. Shift money toward the best-performing states.

Month 4+ · Scaling

$4.5–6k

Increase winning campaigns +30–50%. Build lookalike audiences from closed clients. LinkedIn gets tested only if Meta lead quality stalls.

Break-even math: at a $12.5k average contract, one closed client every 4+ months covers the entire media budget. The forecast says ~2 per month.

The rhythm in one line: month 1 we stay steady and let the algorithms learn; months 2–3 we cut what's weak; from month 4 we only pour more money into what already earns. We never scale a losing ad.
